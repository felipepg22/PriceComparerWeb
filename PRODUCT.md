# Product

## Register

product

## Users
PriceComparerWeb serves shoppers who want to compare offers across online stores before buying. They use it in a decision flow where they need a fast answer on price, seller trust, and whether an offer is worth opening.

## Product Purpose
The product helps users search for a product once and review comparable offers with clear pricing and seller context. Success means users can quickly identify credible options, understand price differences, and move to a chosen offer with confidence.

## Brand Personality
Voice and tone are trustworthy, transparent, and objective, while still modern, calm, and fast. The interface should reduce noise and keep attention on clear decisions rather than promotional pressure.

## Anti-references
This should not look like a coupon site, a noisy marketplace, an ad-heavy comparison portal, or a generic ecommerce homepage. Avoid cluttered promotional UI patterns and aggressive visual urgency cues.

## Design Principles
- Keep search and comparison task-first, with the shortest path from query to decision.
- Make confidence visible by presenting pricing and seller signals clearly and consistently.
- Prefer calm clarity over promotional density, so users can compare without distraction.
- Preserve familiar product-UI affordances so the interface disappears behind the task.
- Communicate objectively in copy and state messaging, prioritizing precision over hype.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Target WCAG 2.2 AA across the product. Ensure strong text contrast, full keyboard navigation for all interactive controls, visible focus states, and reduced-motion-friendly behavior for transitions.
